RECOVERED SHIP'S LOG — ANNO DOMINI 1689

CHRONICLES OF THE DREAD

Three salt-stained pages torn from Captain Malakor Vane’s charcoal ledger, detailing the night the sun drowned beneath the iron swells.

FOLIO I · 14 NOV
LAT 00° 00' N — THE DEAD CALM

I. The Sky That Bled Soot

On the nineteenth night beyond the Cartographer’s Edge, the stars did not set—they were scraped from the firmament like chalk from slate. A charcoal tempest coiled above our mainmast, raining warm ash upon a sea turned thick as printer’s ink.

“The compass needle spun thrice widdershins, then cracked the glass to point straight down into the keel.”
FOLIO II · 29 NOV
DEPTH UNKNOWN — MARROW REACH

II. The Pact of Black Oak

When the rations turned to brine and our canvas shredded into burial shrouds, the hull itself began to breathe. Barnacles of tarnished silver stitched the shattered planks together, fed by the furnace ember burning beneath the quarterdeck.

“She takes no water now, though her ribs are open to the sea. It is the ocean that fears to enter her.”
FOLIO III · NO DAWN
BEARING: BEYOND THE HORIZON

III. The Uncharted Summons

Whenever fog gathers thick along coastal piers and the tide pulls outward against the wind, the Black Dread drops anchor just beyond the harbour bell. Her stern lantern burns for those who hear the abyssal tide.

“Sign no articles in ink. Step across the gangplank, and the sea remembers your name forever.”
